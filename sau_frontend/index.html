Schedule and publish content to every social platform from one dashboard

Socialupload is a web application for creators, agencies, and social media operators. It connects the social accounts you already own and gives you one place to prepare content, queue it for a chosen time, publish it to each platform, and review how it performed. You keep using your own accounts — Socialupload publishes to them on your behalf through each platform's official API or an authenticated browser session.

What you can do with Socialupload

Multi-platform publishing

Upload a video, image, or text post once, then fan it out to the accounts and pages you select. Each platform keeps its own caption, title, hashtags, and visibility settings, so a single piece of content can be tailored per channel instead of copy-pasted.

Scheduling and a publishing queue

Set a publish date and time for each target and let the scheduler release the post automatically. The queue shows what is pending, what has gone out, and what failed, with retries and per-job logs so a failed upload can be diagnosed instead of silently disappearing.

Media and template library

Keep uploaded videos, images, captions, and reusable publishing templates in a library so recurring posts and multi-brand workflows do not require rebuilding the same configuration every time.

Account connections

Connect accounts through each platform's official authorization flow. Stored credentials — OAuth tokens, refresh tokens, and session cookies — live on the server so that scheduled posts can be published while you are offline. You can disconnect an account at any time from the Accounts page, which removes the stored credentials for that account.

Analytics and engagement

Review views, reach, impressions, watch time, and engagement per post and per platform, and manage comments and direct messages for the platforms that support it, so you can see which content is working and respond to your audience.

Supported platforms

TikTok, YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, Threads, Reddit, X (Twitter), Bilibili, Douyin, Xiaohongshu, Kuaishou, WeChat Channel, Baijiahao, Telegram, Discord, Bluesky, and Patreon. Available integrations depend on the platforms you connect and the permissions you grant.

Who it is for

Socialupload is built for adults and authorized business users who manage one or more social accounts — individual creators publishing on a schedule, agencies running several client brands, and teams coordinating a shared content calendar. It is not directed to children.
