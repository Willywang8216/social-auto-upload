Data Deletion Instructions

Socialupload — Last updated: 2026-09-12

This page explains how to delete the data that Socialupload holds about you, and how to revoke Socialupload's access to the social media accounts you connected. If you would rather have us do it for you, use the request method in the last section.

1. Delete data yourself, in the app

  1. Sign in to your Socialupload dashboard at https://socialupload.iamwillywang.com.
  2. Open Accounts and, for each connected account you want to remove, click the ✕ (Remove) button on the account card. This permanently deletes the stored OAuth tokens, refresh tokens, session cookies, profile data, platform user identifiers, and account configuration for that account.
  3. Open Library and Publish Center and delete any uploaded media, drafts, templates, campaigns, or scheduled jobs you want removed.
  4. Open Settings and delete your profile or workspace if that option is available for your account type.

Removing a connected account deletes its credentials from the active database immediately. Records that remain in rolling backups are purged within 30 days.

2. Revoke Socialupload's access on each platform

Removing an account inside Socialupload stops us using its credentials, but the platform itself may still list Socialupload as an authorized app. Revoke it directly there as well:

  • Google / YouTube: Google Account Permissions — select Socialupload and remove access.
  • Facebook / Instagram: Facebook Apps and Websites — remove Socialupload.
  • TikTok: TikTok app → Profile → Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions — revoke Socialupload.
  • X (Twitter): Settings → Security and account access → Apps and sessions → Connected apps — revoke Socialupload.
  • Threads: Threads Settings → connected apps — revoke Socialupload.
  • Reddit: Reddit app preferences — revoke access for Socialupload.
  • Discord / Telegram / Bluesky / Patreon: remove Socialupload from the authorized or connected apps list in that platform's account settings.

3. Request deletion by email

If you cannot sign in, or you want all data associated with a social account deleted, email us and we will process the request within 30 days:

  • Email: willywang8216@gmail.com
  • Subject: Data deletion request
  • Include: the email address or platform username you used with Socialupload, so we can locate the right records.

We will confirm once the data has been deleted. Some records may be retained where the law requires it, or for as long as needed to resolve a dispute or prevent fraud; the Privacy Policy sets out those retention periods.

4. What gets deleted

  • Connected account credentials: OAuth access tokens, refresh tokens, session cookies and browser storage-state files.
  • Account metadata: platform user identifiers, channel or page names, avatar URLs, and granted scopes.
  • Content you uploaded or generated: videos, images, captions, and drafts held in your library.
  • Workflow records: scheduled jobs, queue history, publishing logs, and error records.

Content you already published to a social platform is stored by that platform, and must be deleted there directly — deleting it from Socialupload does not remove the published copy.
